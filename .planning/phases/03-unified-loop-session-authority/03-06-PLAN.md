<objective>
Close the transcript's largest real-world disclosure path: secrets that enter through user text or tool output persist forever and are replayed to every provider. Redaction at admission is the only correct site because the mirror deliberately stores bytes-as-sent.

**User story (MVP enrichment):** **As a** developer who pastes a token or runs a command that prints one, **I want to** have it redacted before it enters my history, **so that** my transcript, its exports and every future request never carry it.

Purpose: LOOP-05's mirror rule means render-time redaction is impossible; the redaction must happen at the two write boundaries. This plan implements both and proves it with a canary sweep.
Output: `admission.py`, `observability.py`, redaction wired into admission and request build, and a store/export/log sweep test.
</objective>
