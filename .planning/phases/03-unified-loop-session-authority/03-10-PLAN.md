<objective>
Make compaction loop-owned and single-sourced: one engine, one lineage, a fail-closed evidence checkpoint, and accounting that is the provider's usage rather than HALO's estimate.

**User story (MVP enrichment):** **As a** developer in a multi-day session, **I want to** have older turns summarised only after their evidence is archived and to see a context percentage that matches what the provider actually accepted, **so that** the agent never silently forgets a stated constraint and my budget numbers are real.

Purpose: LOOP-04 has two failure modes — a second compactor and accounting drift. Both are structural: the checkpoint gate disables every other authority, and usage is recorded rather than derived.
Output: `HaloContextEngine`, `TranscriptArchiveProvider`, the generation-rotation commit, threshold/usage-anchor verification, and the compaction fixture matrix.
</objective>

| Boundary | Description |
