<objective>
**As a** maintainer, **I want to** import the largest and most complex tree — three build graphs, LFS-tracked binaries, and its own `patches/` directory — without unifying anything, **so that** the hardest import is proven and the remaining Phase 1 work is verification rather than import risk.

Doing it in its own plan keeps one failure attributable.

Output: the fourth imported tree with a full ledger, its LFS inventory generated at import time, and watch entries for the RPC wire and process-spawn sites.

Scope note (estimate 110k vs the 100k advisory budget, confidence low): no split is taken. The three coupled surfaces — LFS pointer preservation, three workspace roots, and the watch entries that must be derived from the tree that was just imported — are only measurable in one import; splitting the LFS inventory into its own plan would require a second full import of the largest tree.
</objective>
